1What it audits

01

Starts From What's Decided

Reads your existing rules first and never re-asks

If your project already has a written style guide, or rules saved from an earlier run, those count as decided. Run it again anytime and it only proposes what is new or still inconsistent, so the guide grows over time.

02

The Whole Site at Once

Type, spacing, color and components across every page

It scans all your pages for headings, body and small-print sizes, spacing on phone and desktop, color use, repeated components like cards and headers, disclaimers and dark mode. Anything already scheduled on your to-do list is left alone.

Ratify What You Already Do

Patterns you've settled on become official rules

When the site already does something one way in practice, it is shown once with its exact code, so you can confirm it as the official rule rather than leave it as habit.

3How you decide

12

Four Clear Choices

Deny, revise, fix everywhere now, or record for later

Each item has Deny, Request Revision, Accept – Apply Everywhere Now and Accept – Record for Later. You decide both whether the rule stands and when existing pages catch up.

DenyRequest RevisionAccept – Apply Everywhere NowAccept – Record for Later
13

Works as Designed

Say no and still record why the difference is on purpose

A Works as Designed checkbox on Deny records the current state as the rule, with your comment as the reason. Two things that look inconsistent but are meant to differ get documented instead of flagged again next time.

DenyWorks as Designed
admins get a denser layout on purpose
14

A Note on Anything

An optional comment box on every decision

All four choices take an optional comment. On an Accept it adjusts the rule and the fix, so you never need a separate approve-with-changes button.

also use this on the checkout page

4After you decide

16

Fix It Everywhere Now

Accepted rules can be applied to every page at once

Choose Apply Everywhere Now and every page that does it differently is updated to match, in the same pass, with your comment folded in.

/aboutupdated
/pricingupdated
/blogupdated
17

Or Record It for Later

The rule is saved now and the cleanup goes on your to-do list

Choose Record for Later and the rule is written down immediately, while updating the existing pages becomes a to-do entry naming which pages and what the rule is. It doesn't get lost.

Rule saved
To-do: update 6 pages to new card padding
18

A Living Style Guide

Accepted rules collect in one guide page you keep

Every accepted rule lands in a style guide page in your project, sorted by category, each with its example, its code and a one-line rule. It is republished to the same link every time.

19

Future Sessions Follow It

Your project instructions point new work at the guide

It checks that your project's instructions for Claude point at the style guide, and if they don't, proposes adding the pointer and waits for your go-ahead, since that file is yours. Later work starts from your rules instead of reinventing them.

Project instructions  → follow the style guide
pointer in place
20

Optional: A Hidden Address

Opt-in only: served on your site after you approve

If you want it, the guide can also be served at an unlinked address on your own site, like /styleguide, always showing the saved version. This is optional and opt-in, and it is never set up without your explicit approval.

yoursite.com/styleguide
Opt-in: only with your explicit OK. Not linked from any menu.

Where the Page Goes

A private link, a local file, or your Wi-Fi
