1What you see

01

Spots That Need a Photo

Each place on the page that needs a picture is a slot

The page is broken into slots, one per spot that needs a photo, each with a note on what it should illustrate. You decide slot by slot instead of all or nothing.

02

Real Photos, Not Guesses

Real, properly licensed candidates for every slot

Candidates are real photographs from free-license stock libraries, or official cover images for books. You choose between actual images, not descriptions of them.

B · stock

03

Checked by Eye First

Every candidate is opened and looked at before you see it

Each image is downloaded and looked at before it is offered: the right subject, the right book edition, the right orientation, not a broken placeholder. A link that loads is not taken as proof.

Option A
right subject right orientation not a placeholder
04

Where It Came From

Every candidate shows its source and license

Each card notes where the image came from and why it fits. You know what you are allowed to use before you pick it.

Option B
free license
from a stock library · close-up fits a narrow column
05

Reuse What You Have

Photos already on your site are offered first

If your site already has a photo of the same subject, it is offered as a candidate with an Already on site badge. Pages stay consistent and you avoid two different photos of the same thing.

Option AAlready on site
Option BNew · stock
06

Only Real Contenders

One to three options, never padded

Most slots get one to three candidates. If only one image truly fits, you see just that one rather than filler added to make a set.

Only option
one real match · no filler
07

Your Site's Look

The gallery uses your page's own fonts and colors

The review page borrows the palette and type of the page the photos are for, so you judge each photo in a setting close to where it will live.

09

Four Clear Choices

Keep none, use it, use it with changes, or search again

Each slot has Keep None, Use Selected, Approve with Comment and Request Revision. That covers no photo here, this photo as is, this photo adjusted, or none of these.

Keep NoneUse SelectedApprove with CommentRequest Revision
10

Say How to Use It

Ask for a crop, caption or different placement

Approve with Comment opens a box for how the photo should be used, like a tighter crop, a caption or a different position on the page.

Approve with Comment
crop tighter, add a caption
11

Search Again

None of these? Say what to look for instead

Request Revision means start over for that slot, in a different direction. Your comment says what to try: another subject, mood or source.

One button copies a plain summary headed with the gallery's name, listing each slot's decision, chosen option and comments. You paste it into chat and it can't be confused with another review.

15

Uploaded to Your Image Host

The chosen photo goes to your image host and onto the page

Each accepted photo is uploaded to your image host, following the folder and naming your project notes describe, and its address is wired into the page at the right spot. If the project has no image host yet, Claude asks where images should live before uploading anything.

16

Your Changes Applied

Crops, captions and alt text from your comments

If you approved with a comment, the photo is adjusted before it goes in: cropped, captioned, given alt text or moved, as you asked.

Cropped

17

Only What You Picked

Nothing else on the page gets touched

Only accepted slots get a photo. Kept-empty slots stay empty and slots sent back for another search are left alone until the next round.

No Blank Boxes

Every image is confirmed to load before you see the page

A broken image fails silently as an empty box, so every image on the review page is checked to have actually loaded before it is published to you.

23

Where the Page Goes

A private link, a local file, or your Wi-Fi

Your project picks where review pages go: a private claude.ai link, an HTML file that opens in your browser, or that file served on your home Wi-Fi so a phone can open it. Nothing is ever uploaded or tunneled in the Wi-Fi mode, and the server stops when the review ends.
